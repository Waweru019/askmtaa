Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using AskMtaa as a customer or service provider.

Is using AskMtaa completely free for customers?

Yes! Asking for service providers via our WhatsApp bot or browsing our web directory is 100% free for everyone. You connect directly with vendors without paying any platform fees.

How do service providers get verified on AskMtaa?

Service providers register their business profiles with verified phone numbers, real business locations, and work samples. Customers leave ratings and reviews after using services, helping maintain quality and trust.

How do I list my micro-business on AskMtaa?

Simply click on the Register Your Business link, create an account, select your business category, add your location, and list your services or products. Once approved, customers in your neighborhood will discover you instantly!

Which towns and areas in Kenya are supported?

AskMtaa works across major Kenyan towns including Nairobi, Embu, Thika, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu, and Mombasa, continuously expanding to nearby local estates and neighborhoods.
